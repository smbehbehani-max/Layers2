Privacy policy

Layers of Saudi Arabia · website and apps · effective 7 November 2026

Layers of Saudi Arabia is a map of Saudi Arabia's geology, archaeology and ecology. It is designed so that it keeps no information about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information about you to us or to anyone else.

Location

When you turn on Go live, the app asks your phone for your location so it can show your position on the map and the distance and direction to the nearest place. This happens entirely on your device. Your location is never stored and never transmitted. Turn Go live off, or decline the permission, and the app works normally without it.

Read aloud

The Listen button uses the speech voices built into your phone or browser. The text is spoken on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Settings saved on your device

The app remembers your language, your chosen mode (Explorer or Researcher) and the places you have opened in Explorer mode. These are stored only in your browser or phone storage and can be cleared at any time by clearing the site data or deleting the app.

Third parties

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these services may record standard technical details of a request, such as an IP address, under their own privacy policies: GitHub and Google Fonts. We do not receive this information. Links to research papers and other websites open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page: github.com/smbehbehani-max/Layers-Saudi.
