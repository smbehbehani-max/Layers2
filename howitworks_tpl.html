How GeoHeritage Explorer works

From a new paper to a story on the map: what runs on its own every week, and the one step that stays yours.

1
Automatic

AUTOMATIC · EVERY SUNDAY

Search, read, draft

At 8:56 am Kuwait time a scheduled task looks for new research about the 74 places, reads each source and drafts a bilingual summary with its link.

YOU · ANY TIME

Approve or reject

Drafts wait in a queue only you can see. Nothing reaches viewers until you approve it, and rejected items are never suggested again.

EVERYONE · INSTANTLY

Live in the app

Approved findings appear at once in What’s new and on the place’s card. When you ask, a new place grows into a full entry with a story and cartoon.
